## Brand & Style

The design system projects an uncompromising synthesis of avant-garde Swiss editorial rigor and technical orbit dynamism. Tailored for a software engineer, SaaS founder, and digital creator, the identity conveys intellectual weight, engineering precision, and visionary momentum.

The visual direction balances:
- **70% Experimental Swiss Editorial:** Massive, architectural typography scales, structured structural indices (`01 // ARCHITECTURE`, `02 // VENTURES`), hairline borders, and tactile, stone-tinted negative space.
- **30% Technical Orbit & Electric Energy:** Laser-precise orbital coordinates, glowing micro-accents, deep midnight structural inversions, and high-energy electric blue visual drivers.

The user experience evokes the feel of a high-end physical architectural publication cross-engineered with an aerospace launch console. It avoids tech-startup genericism in favor of high-conviction typography, generous spatial pauses, and precise technical documentation styling.

## Colors

The palette operates on strict volumetric discipline: 75% warm editorial stone neutrals, 15% nocturnal midnight contrasts, and 10% saturated kinetic electric blue.

- **Background & Canvas:** `#F7F7F5` (Parchment Paper). Delivers organic, non-sterile warmth for expansive editorial reading.
- **Surface & Panel Tier:** `#E9E9E7` (Soft Editorial Gray). Used for nested project containers, tabular data backgrounds, and card substrates.
- **Borders & Technical Dividers:** `#D4D4D0` (Subtle Editorial Wireframe) and `#111318` (Structural Master Ruled Lines).
- **Ink / Typography Primary:** `#111318` (Near-Black Pitch). Used for hyper-dense display headlines, body text, and primary narrative blocks.
- **Midnight Structural Inversion:** `#080F24` (Deep Orbital Navy). Applied to full-bleed terminal sections, marquee project spotlights, and the anchoring footer.
- **Electric Accent:** `#2563EB` (ARIS Orbit Blue). Reserved strictly for active focal points, interactive coordinate badges, focus rings, hover indicators, and hyper-dense metadata tags.

## Typography

The typography creates a dramatic contrast between Swiss monumental proportions and scientific monospace telemetry.

### Text Styling Directives
- **Outline & Solid Counterpoint:** Display hero titles frequently pair filled solid weight (`#111318`) with transparent wireframe outline text (`-webkit-text-stroke: 1.5px #111318`) across alternate words to evoke physical typesetting plates.
- **Section Indexes:** Major section categories utilize `label-mono-lg` with zero-padded numerals (`01 //`, `02 //`, `03 //`) in Orbit Blue (`#2563EB`) preceding the primary display titles.
- **Metadata Coordinates:** Timestamps, tech-stack tags, commit references, and geolocation indicators (`KINSHASA [FIH] / 04°19'S 15°19'E`) must strictly render in `Space Mono` uppercase.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an asymmetric 12-column Swiss editorial grid with uncompromising rhythm. 

- **Outer Margins:** Expansive 4rem desktop canvas paddings establish museum-grade breathing space, tapering strictly to 1.25rem on mobile devices.
- **Vertical Intervals:** Large section gaps utilize `space-2xl` (8rem) to create dramatic pauses between portfolio showcases, narrative essays, and interactive modules.
- **Grid Structure:** 
  - **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column layout with 1.5rem gutters. Typical content compositions feature a 4-column contextual metadata rail pinned alongside an 8-column narrative canvas.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with 1.25rem gutters. Sidebars fold cleanly above narrative blocks.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 1rem gutters. All structural grids flatten into a linear vertical sequence with continuous hairline dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects standard fuzzy drop shadows in favor of architectural surface layering, brutalist ghost borders, and precision orbital illumination.

- **Tonal Stepping:** Surfaces stack without drop shadows. The foundation sits at `#F7F7F5`. Elevated interactive zones and cards step up into `#E9E9E7`. Tactical floating bars utilize 90% opacity with an active `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- **Editorial Hairlines:** Structural depth is articulated through 1px crisp borders (`#D4D4D0` on light canvases; `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` inside Midnight surfaces).
- **Orbital Blue Radiance:** Used sparingly for micro-elevation on active triggers and state rings. A subtle ambient glow (`0 0 24px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.25)`) signals primary interactive elements against deep midnight navy backgrounds.

## Shapes

The primary geometry is strictly brutalist and architectural: **Level 0 (Sharp, 0px)**. 

Every card, content container, technical divider, modular accordion row, and tabular index maintains hard 90-degree corners to honor structural Swiss poster graphics.

**Controlled Geometric Exceptions:**
- **Navigation Pill:** The standalone top floating navigation uses full pill rounding (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to produce an ergonomic, orbital instrument aesthetic that detaches completely from the sharp architectural canvas.
- **Orbital Ring Badges:** Radial indicators, status dots, and telemetry glyphs feature perfect circular proportions (1:1 aspect ratio with full radial curvature).

## Components

### Floating Navigation Pill
- **Structure:** Floating horizontal island fixed at the top center of the viewport with a `z-index` of 50.
- **Styling:** Fully rounded pill geometry (`rounded-full`), padded `0.5rem 1.25rem`. Surface is `#111318` at 85% opacity with an ambient `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` perimeter outline.
- **Items:** Links in `Space Mono` 12px uppercase text with smooth transit states. Active status is accompanied by a 6px `#2563EB` pulsing coordinate dot.

### Expansive Showcase Project Cards
- **Geometry:** Hard zero-radius sharp bounds. Full-bleed borders with 1px `#D4D4D0` outer bounds.
- **Composition:** Asymmetric split with an interactive 60% media zone (high-definition product imagery or software canvases) and a 40% technical editorial dossier (project scope, year, stack pills, live build link).
- **Hover State:** Cursor movement initiates a 1px border shift to `#2563EB` and smooth monochromatic-to-color image balance recovery.

### Full-Width Interactive Accordion Rows (Expertise / Venture Matrix)
- **Geometry:** Seamless full-width spans separated by 1px solid hairline dividers (`#D4D4D0`).
- **Closed State:** Minimalist 80px high horizontal strip. Left: Monospace index (`01`, `02`). Center: Massive 36px `Space Grotesk` domain label. Right: Subtle mechanical `+` glyph.
- **Open State:** Shifts background smoothly to `#E9E9E7`. Expands to reveal detailed engineering stacks, architecture blueprints, and active SaaS initiatives alongside an interactive `VIEW CASE STUDY` call-to-action in `#2563EB`.

### Buttons & Technical CTAs
- **Primary Action:** Sharp-cornered (`0px`) container filled with `#111318`. Text rendered in `Space Mono` 12px uppercase in `#F7F7F5`. Hover swaps fill instantly to `#2563EB` with no border-radius transition.
- **Secondary / Wireframe Action:** Sharp-cornered 1px outline of `#111318`. Text in `#111318`. Hover fills `#111318` and turns text `#F7F7F5`.
- **Orbital Coordinate Badge:** Monospace tag with a sharp outline, a 4px inner circle indicator, and strict micro-letter-spacing (`0.08em`).

### Form Inputs & Fields
- **Container:** Rectilinear sharp baseline inputs with minimal background shading (`#E9E9E7`).
- **Borders:** Crisp bottom-only or fully enclosed 1px borders in `#111318`.
- **Focus:** No soft outer halo; transitions directly to a high-contrast 2px `#2563EB` structural ring with a blinking monospace prompt.

### High-Contrast Midnight Structural Footer
- **Canvas:** Full-bleed `#080F24` (Deep Midnight Navy) canvas closing the experience.
- **Content:** Massive outlined hero headline (`LET'S BUILD`), paired with live status telemetry (Kinshasa time zone indicator, current availability status, PGP public key link).
- **Accents:** Active orbital coordinates and social links rendered in `#2563EB` transitioning to `#FFFFFF` on hover.
